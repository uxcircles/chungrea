What we handle

The firm's work areas, as listed on its own site and with the Scottish Legal Aid Board. Ask us if your matter is not here.

  1. 01

    Asylum

    Claims for protection, screening and substantive interviews, fresh claims, and appeals to the First-tier Tribunal.

  2. 02

    Immigration

    Family visas, leave to remain, settlement and citizenship applications, and refusals that need a second look.

  3. 03

    Deportation

    Deportation and removal decisions, detention and bail, and the human rights arguments that can stop removal.

  4. 04

    Judicial review

    Challenging Home Office decisions in the Court of Session. In Scotland the petition normally has to be lodged within three months.

  5. 05

    Personal injury

    Claims for injuries at work, on the road or in public places.

  6. 06

    Notary public

    Certified copies, affidavits and witnessed signatures for documents that will be used abroad.

Time limits worth knowing

14 days
Most in-country appeals to the First-tier Tribunal, from the date the decision was sent
28 days
Most appeals made from outside the UK, from the date you received the decision
3 months
Judicial review petitions to the Court of Session, from the date the grounds arose
