Accessibility

This concept is built to WCAG 2.2 AA: it works with a keyboard and screen readers, text can be enlarged to 200%, colours meet contrast guidelines, and motion is reduced if your device asks for it.

The office is wheelchair accessible and there is parking nearby. There is no induction loop. Home visits are available for clients who are housebound.

If something does not work for you, call 0141 248 2204 or email info@chungrea.com.
