Legal aid covers immigration and asylum work in Scotland

We are registered with the Scottish Legal Aid Board, and immigration, asylum and nationality is one of the case types we take on under legal aid. Whether you qualify depends on your income and savings, and for some work on the merits of the case. We check this with you at the first meeting.
